1. THE SHORT ANSWER
==============================================================================
- Only GOODS and simple SERVICES tenders are realistic. Works (1,601 of 2,043) need a
  PWD/department contractor class registration and experience certificates.
- Within goods and services, choose:
    * Jaipur office, so you can deliver and visit yourself
    * value up to about Rs 25 lakh (below Rs 1 crore is the limit for startup relaxation)
    * "EMD exemption allowed: Yes" and ideally "Tender fee exemption allowed: Yes"
    * RATE CONTRACTS, where you supply on demand over a year (little stock up front)
    * common items you can buy from a Jaipur wholesaler on credit: stationery, bags,
      office items, printing, consumables
    * at least 5 days left to the deadline
- Avoid: drugs, medical and lab items, Discom or power-plant equipment, test instruments,
  manpower supply, security, transport and handling, food and packaging for dairies,
  anything with "installation and commissioning". All of these need licences, OEM
  authorisation, approved-vendor status, fleets or large deposits.
- Of 2,043 tenders, the filters leave 13 worth reading and 7 maybe. Six are in Jaipur
  and are genuinely suitable (section 3).

2. WHAT THE RULES GIVE A NEW COMPANY (Rajasthan)
==============================================================================
RTPP Act 2012 / RTPP Rules 2013 and Finance Department / iStart notifications:
a) iStart-registered STARTUPS: relaxation of prior experience and prior turnover, and
   exemption from bid security (EMD) for goods and services procurement up to Rs 1 crore.
   Works are NOT covered. The tender must say "EMD exemption allowed: Yes" (1,798 of 2,043 do).
   Eligibility (verify on istart.rajasthan.gov.in): Pvt Ltd / LLP / registered partnership,
   registered office in Rajasthan, under 10 years old, turnover under Rs 100 crore.
   A sole proprietorship does NOT qualify.
b) Rajasthan MICRO & SMALL ENTERPRISES (Udyam registration, free, same day):
   50% of the tender document fee, bid security 0.5% instead of 2%, performance
   security 1% (goods), and purchase preference. Proprietorships qualify.
c) QRate-rated startups (iStart Bronze or above) can get DIRECT work orders without
   bidding (up to Rs 25 lakh per order, a few orders per year) from departments. This is the
   strongest zero-experience route if your product or service has an innovation angle.
d) The RISL processing fee (usually Rs 500) is never exempt and is paid on every bid.
e) After you win: performance security (1% for MSE, otherwise up to 5%) and a signed
   agreement. Payment usually comes 30-90 days after delivery and inspection.

Important: the "Pre-qualification" field on the portal usually says "Please refer
tender documents". The real experience and turnover clauses are in the NIT PDF, and those
downloads need the portal captcha, so open them manually. If a clause asks for
past orders or turnover, quote the startup relaxation (RTPP rule / iStart notification) in
a clarification request or at the pre-bid meeting.

4. WHAT TO DO THIS WEEK (deadlines are 02 to 05 Nov)
==============================================================================
Day 1 (all free or cheap):
  [ ] PAN + bank current account in the firm name
  [ ] GST registration (free; most tenders require a GST certificate; 3-7 days)
  [ ] Udyam registration (free, Aadhaar based, instant) -> MSE benefits
  [ ] Class 3 DSC (signing + encryption) on a USB token: about Rs 1,500-2,500, 1 day with video KYC
  [ ] Enrol as bidder on eproc.rajasthan.gov.in (free), map the DSC
Day 2-3:
  [ ] Download the NIT + BOQ for #1 and #2 (captcha, manual). Read eligibility, experience,
      turnover, sample and delivery clauses.
  [ ] Get rates from 2-3 Jaipur wholesalers; ask for 30-60 day credit against a work order.
  [ ] Fill the BOQ with your landed price + 8-15% margin. Rate contracts are L1, the
      lowest price wins.
  [ ] Pay fees/EMD through the e-GRAS challan linked from the bid (or claim exemption with
      the certificate), upload, sign and submit at least a day before the deadline.
Parallel (1-3 weeks, for the next round):
  [ ] Incorporate a Pvt Ltd or LLP (if you are a proprietor now), then DPIIT recognition
      + iStart registration -> EMD exemption + experience/turnover relaxation
  [ ] GeM seller registration (free). Many departments also buy there, and small
      direct-purchase orders are a quick way to build "past supply" proof.
  [ ] If you have an innovative product or service: apply for QRate to unlock
      direct orders up to Rs 25 lakh without bidding.

Money reality with "zero budget":
  - As MSE only (this week): about Rs 2,000 for the DSC plus about Rs 1,000-10,000 per bid.
  - As an iStart startup: DSC + about Rs 500-1,000 per bid (processing + half fee).
  - After winning: 1% performance security and stock until payment. Use supplier
    credit, a MUDRA Shishu/Kishore loan against the work order, or a partner supplier.
    Do not bid one-shot supplies you cannot fund.

  - Winning gives a GOVERNMENT SUPPLY ORDER and completion certificate, which is the

    Tender fee (MSE half)        Rs 295
    Processing fee               Rs 500
    EMD                          Rs 0 if exemption certificate accepted, else Rs 9,000 (MSE)
    Total                        about Rs 2,300 (with exemption) to Rs 12,000 (without)
  After winning (loan):
    Performance security 1% (MSE) to 5%   Rs 18,000 - 90,000 (refunded after the contract)
    Agreement stamp paper                 as per NIT
    Stock float: about Rs 1.5 L of orders per month x 2-3 months before payment
                                          Rs 3 - 4.5 lakh revolving
    Loan size to ask for                  Rs 5 lakh working capital
  Margin reality: stationery is bid item by item and the lowest rate wins, so expect 5-10% net
  (about Rs 90,000 - 1.8 lakh on a full Rs 18 L of orders). The real gain is the experience.

Best loans for this (apply NOW, with the work order later as support):
  1) Mukhyamantri Yuva Swarozgar Yojana (MYSY), Rajasthan, launched 12-Jan-2026, valid to 2029
     - INTEREST-FREE (100% interest reimbursed by the state) + margin money + CGTMSE fee
       reimbursed. Trade/service: up to Rs 5 L for graduates/ITI, Rs 3.5 L for 8th-12th pass.
     - Age 18-45, Rajasthan domicile, not a bank defaulter; firms need 51%+ ownership by
       18-45-year-olds. Apply on SSO > Citizen Apps > "Mukhyamantri Yuva Swarozgar (MYSY)"
       or at an e-Mitra. The DIC forwards it to your chosen bank at its weekly meeting.
     - Documents: Aadhaar, domicile (mool niwas), photo, education certificate, project
       report (DPR), bank details.
  2) PM MUDRA Kishore (Rs 50,000 - 5 lakh), collateral-free, about 9-12% interest, via any bank or
     jansamarth.in. A good backup if MYSY is slow or you are over 45.
  3) Bank overdraft/BG limit under CGTMSE guarantee for the performance security.
  Note: TReDS invoice discounting is for central PSU/corporate buyers, not JMC.

Risks to check before bidding:
  - JMC is a municipal body and payments can be slow. Keep the loan sized for 3 months.
  - Rates are fixed for 365 days while paper prices may rise. Quote with a buffer.
  - Branded items (if the BOQ names brands) need a dealer rate. Ask the wholesaler.
  - No guaranteed quantity: JMC may order much less than Rs 18 L.
  - Late delivery penalties. Read eproc04dcstore.pdf.
